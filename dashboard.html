Morning Brief

Run every morning
"Every morning I type one word and Claude reads my inbox, my calendar, and my projects."
Pulls today's events from your calendar, open tasks from Notion and Microsoft Planner, and outputs a focused 3–5 item priority list. Ready in under 2 minutes.

Parallel Workers

Use for speed
"One drafts, another researches, another edits. All happening at once."
Spawns three sub-agents simultaneously — researcher, drafter, and editor — then combines their outputs into a polished result. Replace <task> with what you need.
Multi-agent Parallel Any task
/parallel-workers <task>

Process Backlog

Run before you sign off
"At night I set Claude to process my backlog while I sleep. Everything is ready by morning."
Sweeps your Notion and Planner queues, drafts email replies, summarizes unread documents, and closes stale tasks. Outputs a clean report of what was handled and what still needs you.
Notion Microsoft Planner Email drafts
/process-backlog

Task to Skill

Whenever you repeat yourself
"Any task I do more than once, I turn into a skill. One word, and Claude handles the rest."
Describe any workflow you keep repeating and this skill writes a new SKILL.md for you. Next time it's a single command — no prompt, no setup. Describe your task in quotes.
Meta-skill Automation Any workflow
/task-to-skill "<describe task>"
