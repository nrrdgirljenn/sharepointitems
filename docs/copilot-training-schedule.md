**Period:** April 28, 2026 – June 30, 2026

---

## Research Basis: Optimal Scheduling for Maximum Attendance

Based on 2025–2026 corporate webinar and training research:

| Factor | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Best day** | **Tuesday** (50% live attendance — highest of any weekday) |
| **Runner-up days** | Wednesday, Thursday (Tue–Thu block = 76% of all successful training events) |
| **Avoid** | Monday (low engagement, heavy internal meetings), Friday (attention drops sharply) |
| **Top morning slot** | **10:00 AM** (49% attendance, strong cognitive engagement) |
| **Top afternoon slot** | **2:00 PM** (53% attendance — highest of any time of day) |
| **Avoid** | 12:00 PM lunch hour (cameras off, 47% lower post-session engagement) |
| **Holiday conflicts** | Memorial Day — Mon May 25 (skip); Juneteenth — Fri Jun 19 (skip) |

**Recommendation applied:** Two slots offered per session day (10:00 AM + 2:00 PM) on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday each week. This gives each participant maximum scheduling flexibility while concentrating sessions in the highest-traffic windows.

## Scheduling Priority Tiers

If you need to prioritize which slots to fill first, rank them in this order:

1. **Tier 1 — Highest traffic:** Tuesday 2:00 PM (research shows 53% live attendance rate)
2. **Tier 2 — Strong traffic:** Tuesday 10:00 AM (49%), Wednesday 2:00 PM (53% equivalent)
3. **Tier 3 — Good traffic:** Wednesday 10:00 AM, Thursday 2:00 PM
4. **Tier 4 — Fill-in:** Thursday 10:00 AM, Monday Jun 29 slots (end-of-period exception)
